# Detailed Analysis of Major Technical Comments

## 1. Voting Methods & Secret Ballot Safeguards (Ted Swiecki Analysis)
**Issue:** Concerns were raised regarding potential peer pressure or intimidation during public votes on controversial forest health policy issues or contested officer elections.

**Resolution in Bylaws (`Section 6.02` & `Section 5.05`):**
* **Routine Decisions:** Voice vote, show of hands, or digital polling for fast consensus.
* **Controversial Matters:** Added an explicit procedural mechanism under *Robert's Rules of Order* where any voting member can move for a confidential (secret) ballot or anonymous electronic vote.
