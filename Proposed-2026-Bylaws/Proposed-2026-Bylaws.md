# BYLAWS OF THE CALIFORNIA FOREST PEST COUNCIL
A CALIFORNIA PUBLIC BENEFIT CORPORATION

## PREAMBLE

The California Forest Pest Council traces its origins to the California Forest Pest Control Action Council, organized in 1951 to coordinate forest pest detection, reporting, and management efforts among public agencies, private landowners, and forestry professionals throughout California.

The Council continues this tradition by bringing together representatives of public agencies, private industry, academic institutions, Tribal governments, landowners, researchers, educators, and other interested parties to promote and protect the health of California's forests.

## Article I. NAME AND OBJECTIVES

**Section 1.01 NAME**  
The name of this organization is the California Forest Pest Council (herein referred to as the "Council"), a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation. 

**Section 1.02 OBJECTIVES**  
The objectives of the Council are to:
1. Coordinate the detection, reporting, and compilation of pest damage.
2. Evaluate pest conditions.
3. Make recommendations on pest control to forest management and protection agencies and forest landowners.
4. Review policy, legal, educational, and research aspects of forest pest management and submit recommendations thereon to appropriate authorities.
5. Foster educational work on forest pests and forest health.

**Section 1.03 ADVISORY ROLE**  
The Council is recognized by the California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection as an advisory body with respect to forest health protection matters.

## Article II. MEMBERSHIP

**Section 2.01 ELIGIBILITY FOR MEMBERSHIP**  
Membership in the Council is open to individuals and organizational representatives with an interest in forest health, forest pest management, or related natural resource fields. Members are expected to support the Council's mission and abide by the Council's Code of Ethics.

**Section 2.02 MEMBERSHIP RECORDS**  
The Council shall maintain a roster of current members. Prospective members may be asked to provide information necessary to establish and maintain membership records in a manner approved by the Executive Committee.

**Section 2.03 VOTING ELIGIBILITY**  
Membership and participation in Council activities are open to interested individuals and stakeholders. Eligibility to vote on Council matters shall be determined in accordance with Article VI (Voting Rights and Eligibility).

**Section 2.04 SUSPENSION OR TERMINATION OF MEMBERSHIP**

**(a) Grounds:**  
Membership, including any associated voting privileges, may be suspended or terminated for conduct inconsistent with the Council's Code of Ethics, bylaws, or mission.

**(b) Procedures:**  
The Council shall seek to address concerns through discussion and resolution whenever practical. Before suspension or termination becomes effective, the member shall be informed of the concern and provided an opportunity to respond. Suspension or termination shall require approval by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the Executive Committee members present and voting.

**(c) Interim Action:**  
If circumstances warrant, the Chairperson may temporarily limit a member's participation in Council activities pending review by the Executive Committee. Any such action shall be reviewed by the Executive Committee at its next meeting or as soon as reasonably practicable.

## Article III. OFFICERS AND MEMBERS-AT-LARGE

**Section 3.01 OFFICERS AND TERMS**  
The Council shall elect from its membership for a two-year term of office a Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and three (3) Members-at-Large.

**Section 3.02 DUTIES OF THE CHAIRPERSON**  
The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Council and the Executive Committee and shall perform any other duties or functions authorized by the Executive Committee. The Chairperson may establish temporary Ad Hoc committees and appoint their chairpersons, subject to ratification by the Executive Committee. The Chairperson serves as an ex-officio, non-voting member of all other standing or ad hoc committees of the Council.

The Council Chairperson, or an Executive Committee member or authorized designee appointed by the Chairperson, shall represent the Council, report forest pest conditions and recommendations of the Council to the California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection, and appear on behalf of the Council before other organizations as appropriate.

**Section 3.03 DUTIES OF VICE-CHAIRPERSON**  
In the event the Chairperson is absent or is unable or unwilling to perform their duties, the Vice-Chairperson shall perform all the duties of the Chairperson.

**Section 3.04 DUTIES OF SECRETARY**  
The Secretary shall be custodian of the records and keep the original and a copy of these Bylaws, a book of minutes of all meetings of the Executive Committee and Council business meetings, and a copy of all conditions reports, proceedings, and publications distributed by the Council.

The Secretary shall take minutes at Executive Committee and Council business meetings, distribute them to the Executive Committee within thirty (30) days following the meeting, ensure meeting notices are distributed in a timely fashion, and maintain the current membership list.

**Section 3.05 DUTIES OF TREASURER**  
The Treasurer shall receive all payments, serve as custodian of Council funds, keep an account of all monies received and expended, and make commitments and disbursements authorized by the Executive Committee. At the annual business meeting and when requested by the Executive Committee, the Treasurer shall make a full report covering the financial affairs of the Council. All funds, books, records, and vouchers under the Treasurer's control shall be subject to inspection and verification by the Executive Committee. The Treasurer shall deliver to their successor all books, records, money, and accounts pertaining to or belonging to the Council.

**Section 3.06 NON-LIABILITY OF OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS**  
The Officers, Executive Committee members, and committee chairs shall not be personally liable for the debts, liabilities, or other obligations of the Council.

**Section 3.07 INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE**  
To the fullest extent permitted by California law, the Council shall indemnify its Officers, Executive Committee members, committee chairs, and authorized agents acting in good faith and in a manner they reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Council. The Council may maintain appropriate Errors & Omissions (E&O) insurance or professional liability coverage for the protection of its leadership and agents against claims arising from acts, errors, omissions, or performance of duties undertaken on behalf of the Council.

**Section 3.08 VACANCIES**  
Vacancies in any elected office or Member-at-Large position may be filled by vote of the Executive Committee for the unexpired portion of the term until the next regular election.

## Article IV. COMMITTEES

**Section 4.01 COMMITTEES**  
The Council shall conduct its activities through Technical Committees, Administrative Committees, Ad Hoc committees, Task Forces, and the Executive Committee (collectively referred to as "Committees"). 

To maintain operational flexibility, the Executive Committee may establish, restructure, consolidate, rename, or dissolve Committees as necessary to fulfill the purposes of the Council.

**Section 4.02 COMMITTEE REPRESENTATION ON EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE**  
The Chairperson of each standing Technical Committee, Administrative Committee, and active Task Force shall serve as its designated representative on the Executive Committee. No individual shall cast more than one vote on the Executive Committee, regardless of the number of leadership roles held.

**Section 4.03 STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES**  
Each Committee may adopt and maintain its own Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines, or charters to govern its internal activities, meetings, and leadership elections, provided such procedures do not conflict with these Bylaws and are approved by the Executive Committee.

**Section 4.04 SUBGROUP STRUCTURE AND TRACKS**  
The structure of Council subgroups shall align with their intended function:

**(a) Technical / Elected Track:** Technical Committees shall have open membership and elect their own leadership.  
**(b) Administrative / Appointed Track:** Administrative Committees shall have their leadership appointed by the Executive Committee.  
**(c) Task Forces and Ad Hoc Committees:** Upon establishing a Task Force or Ad Hoc committee, the Executive Committee shall designate its leadership structure under either the elected or appointed track.

**Section 4.05 TECHNICAL COMMITTEES**  
The Council shall maintain Technical Committees addressing core forest health priorities, including forest insects, forest diseases, invasive vegetation, animal damage, and Southern California forest health issues.

**Section 4.06 ADMINISTRATIVE COMMITTEES**  
The Council shall maintain the following Administrative Committees:

**(a) The Editorial Committee:** Responsible for arranging the preparation and publication of the annual forest pest conditions report, proceedings of the annual meeting, and other official publications. The Editorial Committee chair shall preferably be designated by the Director of the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection.  
**(b) The Conference Committee:** Responsible for planning and executing the annual meeting of the California Forest Pest Council, including programming, local arrangements, registration, and publicity.

**Section 4.07 TASK FORCES**  
Task Forces address specific, complex, or ongoing forest health issues (e.g., Firewood Task Force, Pitch Canker Task Force) and operate as standing operational bodies dedicated to a focused area of concern.

**Section 4.08 AD HOC COMMITTEES**  
Ad Hoc committees are temporary bodies created for a specific task and shall automatically dissolve upon submitting their final findings or completing their designated objective.

## ARTICLE V. THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

**Section 5.01 ROLE, DUTIES, AND AUTHORITY**  
The Executive Committee manages the operational affairs of the Council between membership meetings, coordinates the activities of the Council and its committees, reviews recommendations for Council action, oversees Council finances and grant administration, and serves as the official voice of the Council.

When taking action on matters within the scope of a specific committee, the Executive Committee shall seek input from the affected committee prior to taking action whenever practical.

For purposes of the California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law, the Executive Committee serves as the Board of Directors of the corporation.

**Section 5.02 COMPOSITION AND APPOINTMENTS**

**(a) Officers:**  
The Officers of the Council (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer).

**(b) Committee Representatives:**  
The Chairperson of each Technical Committee, Administrative Committee, and Task Force.

**(c) Members-at-Large:**  
Three (3) Members-at-Large elected by the Council.

**(d) Agency and Industry Representation Appointments:**  
In the event the membership of the Executive Committee set forth in subsections (a) through (c) does not include at least one (1) representative each from:
1. The California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE);
2. The Pacific Southwest Region of the USDA Forest Service;
3. The University of California; and
4. Private industry;

the Chairperson is authorized to appoint an additional person or persons as necessary to ensure representation from these organizations.

**(e) Alternates:**  
Each Executive Committee member may designate an alternate to attend an Executive Committee meeting on their behalf in the event they cannot attend. Because the Executive Committee serves as the Board of Directors under California law, designated alternates may participate in discussions but shall **not** have voting privileges and shall **not** count toward establishing a quorum.

**Section 5.03 SCOPE OF AUTHORITY AND OPERATIONAL LIMITS**  
To ensure efficient management while preserving fundamental corporate governance:

**(a) Executive Committee Scope:**  
The Executive Committee has full authority to:
1. Direct Council operations, manage grants, oversee corporate assets, and manage discretionary spending;
2. Adopt, amend, and enforce Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to govern overall Council operations, including financial approval thresholds for Executive Committee actions;
3. Review and approve Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines, and charters developed by sub-committees, working groups, or task forces;
4. Adopt official Council public policy positions, technical recommendations, public statements, and formal resolutions, or, at the Executive Committee’s discretion, submit significant policy matters to a vote of the general membership pursuant to Section 8.01(b);
5. Appoint committee chairs and fill interim leadership vacancies as authorized by these Bylaws; and
6. Take emergency action on behalf of the Council when immediate action is necessary to protect the Council’s interests.

**(b) Reserved Membership Authority:**  
The Executive Committee shall **not** take the following actions without approval by a vote of the general membership:
1. Amending or repealing these Bylaws or the Articles of Incorporation;
2. Electing Officers and Members-at-Large; or
3. Approving major structural corporate actions, such as a merger, dissolution, major reorganization, or selling/disposing of substantially all Council assets.

**Section 5.04 VOTING AND QUORUM**  
Each defined member of the Executive Committee shall be entitled to one (1) vote.

A quorum shall consist of fifty percent (50%) of the voting members of the Executive Committee. Unless otherwise provided in these Bylaws, actions of the Executive Committee shall be decided by a simple majority (50% plus one) vote of the voting members present.

**Section 5.05 ACTION WITHOUT A MEETING AND ELECTRONIC VOTING**  
**(a) Action by Written Consent:**  
Any action required or permitted to be taken by the Executive Committee may be taken without a meeting if all voting members of the Executive Committee consent in writing (including electronic mail) to the action.

**(b) Electronic Meetings and Email Votes:**  
In lieu of a physical meeting, the Chairperson may call an electronic or email vote on urgent or routine operational matters. Provided that a quorum of the Executive Committee submits written responses or votes via electronic transmission within a specified response window, the action shall be decided by a simple majority vote of those responding. All written consents, ballots, and electronic votes shall be filed with the minutes of the Executive Committee.

## Article VI. VOTING RIGHTS

**Section 6.01 VOTING ELIGIBILITY**  
Voting eligibility is limited to members who agree to abide by the Council's Code of Ethics. Eligibility shall remain valid for two (2) years and may be established by either of the following:

**(a) Attendance:**  
Attendance during the preceding two (2) years at a Council meeting, Technical or Administrative Committee meeting, field session, workshop, conference, or other Council-sponsored activity; or

**(b) Eligibility Request:**  
Submission of a written or electronic request for voting eligibility to the Secretary, or same-day registration at a Council meeting, demonstrating an interest or involvement in forest health, forestry, natural resource management, pest management, research, education, fire protection, or related fields.

**Section 6.02 VOTING RULES**  
Each eligible member shall be entitled to one (1) vote on each matter submitted to the general membership. Unless otherwise provided in these Bylaws, actions of the membership shall be decided by a majority vote of the voting members present.

**(a) Standard Voting:**  
Voting at meetings shall generally be conducted by voice vote, show of hands, or appropriate electronic means. Any member may demand a verification of a voice vote by a show of hands. A formal, counted vote may be ordered by the Chairperson or by a majority vote of the members present.

**(b) Ballot or Recorded Voting:**  
A vote shall be conducted by confidential ballot or recorded roll call if a motion to do so is made, seconded, and approved by a majority of the voting members present. The motion must specify whether the vote shall be confidential (anonymous) or recorded (identified). Ballots may be cast physically or through secure electronic platforms that support the requested method. Such a motion must be made before the final result of the standard vote is officially announced by the Chairperson.

**Section 6.03 ROSTERS**  
The official roster of eligible voting members shall be maintained by the Council Secretary. To accommodate fluid participation, this roster is maintained as an ongoing record and may be updated during Council meetings to immediately grant voting rights to attendees who establish eligibility on-site.

Each Technical and Administrative Committee shall maintain an ongoing roster of members participating in or interested in its subject area. Committees shall promptly provide roster updates to the Council Secretary following meetings, field sessions, or workshops.

## Article VII. MEETINGS

**Section 7.01 NOTICE OF MEETINGS**  
The Council Secretary shall prepare and distribute written or electronic notices of full Council meetings to the membership at least fifteen (15) days prior to the meeting date.

Committee Chairpersons or Secretaries shall handle distribution of notices for their respective committee meetings to interested participants, sending copies to members of the Executive Committee.

**Section 7.02 FULL COUNCIL MEETINGS AND QUORUM**  
The full Council shall meet at least annually in the Fall for its regular business meeting. The eligible voting members present at any properly noticed Council business meeting shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

**Section 7.03 COMMITTEE MEETINGS**  
Technical Committees shall meet at least annually, prior to the annual Council meeting, to review pest conditions in their subject area and to develop technical findings and recommendations.

**Section 7.04 EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE MEETINGS**  
Regular meetings of the Executive Committee shall be held periodically throughout the year and prior to the Annual Council Meeting to review operations, manage Council business, and coordinate recommendations being submitted by committees.

**Section 7.05 CONDUCT OF MEETINGS**  
Meetings shall be presided over by the appropriate Chairperson or designated representative. Meetings shall be governed by the current edition of *Robert’s Rules of Order, Newly Revised*, except where such rules are inconsistent with the Articles of Incorporation or these Bylaws.

**Section 7.06 MINUTES AND RECORDS**  
The Council Secretary shall prepare and distribute minutes of full Council meetings and Executive Committee meetings within thirty (30) days following each meeting to members of the Executive Committee. Copies of minutes may be distributed to general members with meeting notices or at subsequent meetings. 

The Chairpersons or Secretaries of Technical and Administrative Committees shall promptly transmit copies of committee minutes, reports, and official correspondence to the Council Secretary.

## Article VIII. ADVOCACY AND POLICY POSITIONS

**Section 8.01 RESOLUTIONS AND POLICY STATEMENTS**  
Resolutions and official policy statements are the primary means by which the Council advocates action or states technical positions on forest pest and forest health matters.

**(a) Standard Adoption Process:**  
Resolutions shall typically be drafted and introduced in the appropriate subject-area committee, task force, or working group. The committee shall forward proposed resolutions to the Executive Committee for review. In alignment with Section 5.03, final adoption of official Council resolutions and public policy positions generally rests with the Executive Committee.

**(b) Discretionary Referral to General Membership:**  
Notwithstanding subsection (a), if the Executive Committee determines that a proposed resolution, policy position, or public statement involves a matter of major significance, controversy, or broad interest to the Council, the Executive Committee may, by majority vote, refer the matter to the full eligible voting membership for a vote at a regular business meeting, special meeting, or via electronic ballot.

**(c) Statutory & Compliance Limits:**  
Resolutions shall include the names and contact information of intended recipients. All advocacy and resolutions shall strictly comply with the lobbying and political intervention restrictions set forth in Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code and the Articles of Incorporation.

**Section 8.02 LETTERS OF SUPPORT**  
Official Letters of Support may be drafted and issued provided the subject matter strictly aligns with the Council’s established purposes, existing positions, or ongoing initiatives. Requests for Letters of Support shall be submitted to the Executive Committee for review and approval by a simple majority vote. Approved letters may be signed by the Council Chairperson or an authorized designee.

## Article IX. FINANCES

**Section 9.01 EXPENDITURES AND DISBURSEMENTS**  
The Executive Committee shall oversee and authorize expenditures of Council funds. All disbursements, including checks, electronic funds transfers, and commitments of funds, shall be signed or documented by the Treasurer, Council Chairperson, or another officer designated by the Executive Committee in accordance with established Executive Committee Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Single-officer approval thresholds and dual-signature requirements for higher transactions shall be defined within Executive Committee operating procedures.

**Section 9.02 DEPOSITS**  
All funds of the Council shall be deposited promptly to the credit of the Council in such banks, trust companies, or other depositories as selected by the Executive Committee.

**Section 9.03 CONTRACTS AND GRANTS**  
The Executive Committee may authorize any officer or agent to execute contracts, grant agreements, or formal instruments on behalf of the Council. No officer or agent shall have power to bind the Council to any contract without prior Executive Committee authorization or SOP guidelines.

**Section 9.04 GIFTS AND CONTRIBUTIONS**  
The Chairperson or Treasurer may accept unencumbered financial contributions or gifts on behalf of the Council. Any gift or bequest consisting of non-liquid assets requiring liquidation or ongoing management (e.g., real estate, equipment, physical property) must be reviewed and approved by the Executive Committee prior to acceptance.

**Section 9.05 FISCAL YEAR**  
The fiscal year of the Council shall begin on the 1st day of January and end on the 31st day of December.

**Section 9.06 COMPENSATION AND REIMBURSEMENT**  
Officers, Executive Committee members, and committee chairs shall serve without compensation for their governance services. The Executive Committee or Chairperson may authorize reimbursement of reasonable, actual incidental expenses incurred in support of Council business, subject to established operating procedures.

## Article X. AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS OR ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION

**Section 10.01 AMENDMENT PROCEDURES**  
Proposed amendments to these Bylaws or the Articles of Incorporation shall first be submitted to the Executive Committee for review. Upon review, proposed amendments shall be distributed to eligible voting members at least fifteen (15) days prior to the next regular Council business meeting.

These Bylaws may be amended by a majority vote of the eligible voting members present at a properly noticed Council business meeting. Amendments to the Articles of Incorporation require prior approval by the Executive Committee before submission to the voting membership for adoption by majority vote, and remain subject to statutory filing requirements under California law.
