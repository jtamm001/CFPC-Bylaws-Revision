<u>**The California Forest Pest Council traces its origins to the California Forest Pest Control Action Council, organized in 1951 to coordinate forest pest detection, reporting, and management efforts among public agencies, private landowners, and forestry professionals throughout California.**</u>

<u>**The Council continues this tradition by bringing together representatives of public agencies, private industry, academic institutions, Tribal governments, landowners, researchers, educators, and other interested parties to promote and protect the health of California's forests.**</u>

### ARTICLE I. NAME AND OBJECTIVES

**Section 1.01 NAME**
The name of this organization is the California Forest Pest Council (herein referred to as the "Council"), <u>**a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation.**</u>

**Section 1.02 OBJECTIVES**
The objectives of the Council are to:
1. Coordinate the detection, reporting, and compilation of pest damage.
2. Evaluate pest conditions.
3. Make recommendations on pest control to forest management and protection agencies and forest landowners.
4. Review policy, legal, <u>**educational,**</u> and research aspects of forest pest management and submit recommendations thereon to appropriate authorities.
5. Foster educational work on forest pests and forest health.

**Section 1.03 ADVISORY ROLE**
The Council is recognized by the ~~State~~ <u>**California**</u> Board of Forestry <u>**and Fire Protection**</u> as ~~the~~ <u>**an**</u> advisory body ~~to it~~ with respect to forest health protection matters~~, and is a participating member of the Western Forest Pest Committee of the Western Forestry and Conservation Association~~.

### ARTICLE II. MEMBERSHIP & ETHICS

**Section 2.01 ELIGIBILITY FOR MEMBERSHIP**
~~Participation in~~ <u>**Membership in**</u> the Council is open to <u>**individuals and organizational representatives**</u> ~~anyone~~ having an interest in forest health~~ protection~~, <u>**forest pest management, or related natural resource fields.**</u> ~~There is only one class of membership and all members have the same voting rights and privileges.~~ <u>**Members are expected to support the Council's mission and abide by the Council's Code of Ethics.**</u>

<u>**Section 2.02 MEMBERSHIP RECORDS**</u>
<u>**The Council shall maintain a roster of current members. Prospective members may be asked to provide information necessary to establish and maintain membership records in a manner approved by the Executive Committee.**</u>

<u>**Section 2.04 SUSPENSION OR TERMINATION OF MEMBERSHIP**</u>
<u>**(a) Grounds:** Membership, including any associated voting privileges, may be suspended or terminated for conduct inconsistent with the Council's Code of Ethics, bylaws, or mission.</u>
<u>**(b) Procedures:** The Council shall seek to address concerns through discussion and resolution whenever practical. Before suspension or termination becomes effective, the member shall be informed of the concern and provided an opportunity to respond. Suspension or termination shall require approval by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the Executive Committee members present and voting.</u>
