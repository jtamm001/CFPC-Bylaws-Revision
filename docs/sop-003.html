California Forest Pest Council: 2026 Governance Framework & Bylaws Revision

SOP-003: Conditions Report Draft

ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

  1. Purpose: This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes Section 4.06(a) and Section 7.06 of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It establishes formal guidelines for compiling, technical editing, publishing, and distributing the annual California Forest Pest Conditions Report and official meeting summaries.
  2. Governing Scope: Applies to the Editorial Committee, Technical Committee Chairs (Insects, Diseases, Invasive Vegetation, Animal Damage, Southern California), agency liaisons, and contributing forest health specialists.

ARTICLE II. EDITORIAL COMMITTEE LEADERSHIP & RESPONSIBILITIES

  1. Chairperson Appointment (§ 4.06a): The Editorial Committee Chair shall preferably be a professional representative designated by the Director of the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE).
  2. Core Responsibilities:
    • Establish annual data submission deadlines and standard reporting templates for technical contributors.
    • Conduct technical peer review and copyediting of all sub-committee condition summaries.
    • Manage document layout, spatial maps, photo permissions, and publication formatting.
    • Coordinate print and digital publication distribution to stakeholders, state regulators, and public archives.

ARTICLE III. REPORT STRUCTURE AND CONTENT STANDARDS

The annual California Forest Pest Conditions Report shall maintain a standardized, professional structure:

  1. Executive Summary & Key Trends: High-level narrative highlighting major mortality events, drought/climate interactions, and emerging invasive pest threats across California.
  2. Technical Committee Sections:
    • Forest Insects: Bark beetles, defoliators, wood borers, and invasive insect pests.
    • Forest Diseases: Native root diseases, foliar pathogens, cankers, and invasive pathogens (e.g., Phytophthora ramorum, Pitch Canker).
    • Invasive & Undesirable Vegetation: Noxious weed spreads, brush competition, and management impacts.
    • Animal Damage: Porcupine, bear, ungulate, and rodent damage trends.
    • Southern California Forest Health: Regional drought, pest interactions, and urban-wildland interface priorities.
  3. Survey & Remote Sensing Data: Maps, aerial detection survey (ADS) data summaries, and ground-truth plot findings provided by CAL FIRE and USDA Forest Service Forest Health Protection (FHP).
  4. Directory & Resources: List of Council Officers, Committee Chairs, agency contact directories, and links to pest diagnostic services.

ARTICLE IV. ANNUAL PUBLICATION TIMELINE AND WORKFLOW

To ensure timely delivery to the California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection and the general membership, the publication workflow follows a strict annual schedule:

Phase Target Window Milestones & Action Items Responsible Party
Phase 1: Call for Data August 15 Issue standard data collection forms and photo submission guidelines. Editorial Committee Chair
Phase 2: Draft Submissions October 15 Technical Committee Chairs submit draft condition narratives and ADS maps. Technical Committee Chairs
Phase 3: Technical Review October 16 – Nov 10 Peer review, cross-verification of pest data, formatting, and cartography. Editorial Committee
Phase 4: Annual Presentation Fall Annual Meeting Present preliminary conditions highlights at the Annual CFPC Meeting. Editorial Chair / Agency Designees
Phase 5: Final Layout December 15 Final proofreading, indexing, and PDF layout compilation. Editorial Team
Phase 6: Distribution January 31 Digital release, archival filing, and submission to Board of Forestry. Secretary / Editorial Chair

ARTICLE V. DISTRIBUTION, ARCHIVING, AND REPOSITORY MANAGEMENT

  1. Official Distribution List: Upon publication, the Editorial Committee and Council Secretary shall distribute copies (digital PDF or print) to:
    • Executive Committee members and general CFPC membership.
    • The California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection (Bylaws § 1.03).
    • Director of CAL FIRE and Regional Forester, USDA Forest Service Region 5.
    • University Libraries (UC, CSU), Tribal Natural Resources Departments, and state digital archives.
  2. Web Repository: The Council Secretary shall ensure the final PDF report is permanently archived on the official CFPC website and accessible to the public free of charge.

ARTICLE VI. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CITATION STANDARDS

  1. Photo & Data Permissions: All photographs, figures, and spatial maps included in the report must include explicit attribution to the author/agency. Uncredited stock images are strictly prohibited.
  2. Suggested Citation Format:
    California Forest Pest Council. (Year). California Forest Pest Conditions [Year]. Official Report of the California Forest Pest Council, Sacramento, CA. [Pages].
