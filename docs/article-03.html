Article III. OFFICERS AND MEMBERS-AT-LARGE

Section 3.01 OFFICERS AND TERMS

The Council shall elect from its membership for a two-year term of office a Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and three (3) Members-at-Large.

Section 3.02 DUTIES OF THE CHAIRPERSON The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Council and the Executive Committee and shall perform any other duties or functions authorized by the Executive Committee. The Chairperson may establish temporary Ad Hoc committees and appoint their chairpersons, subject to ratification by the Executive Committee. The Chairperson serves as an ex-officio, non-voting member of all other standing or ad hoc committees of the Council.

The Council Chairperson, or an Executive Committee member or authorized designee appointed by the Chairperson, shall represent the Council, report forest pest conditions and recommendations of the Council to the California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection, and appear on behalf of the Council before other organizations as appropriate.

Section 3.03 DUTIES OF VICE-CHAIRPERSON In the event the Chairperson is absent or is unable or unwilling to perform their duties, the Vice-Chairperson shall perform all the duties of the Chairperson.

Section 3.04 DUTIES OF SECRETARY The Secretary shall be custodian of the records and keep the original and a copy of these Bylaws, a book of minutes of all meetings of the Executive Committee and Council business meetings, and a copy of all conditions reports, proceedings, and publications distributed by the Council. The Secretary shall take minutes at Executive Committee and Council business meetings, distribute them to the Executive Committee within thirty (30) days following the meeting, ensure meeting notices are distributed in a timely fashion, and maintain the current membership list.

Section 3.05 DUTIES OF TREASURER The Treasurer shall receive all payments, serve as custodian of Council funds, keep an account of all monies received and expended, and make commitments and disbursements authorized by the Executive Committee. At the annual business meeting and when requested by the Executive Committee, the Treasurer shall make a full report covering the financial affairs of the Council. All funds, books, records, and vouchers under the Treasurer’s control shall be subject to inspection and verification by the Executive Committee. The Treasurer shall deliver to their successor all books, records, money, and accounts pertaining to or belonging to the Council.

Section 3.06 NON-LIABILITY OF OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS The Officers, Executive Committee members, and committee chairs shall not be personally liable for the debts, liabilities, or other obligations of the Council.

Section 3.07 INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE To the fullest extent permitted by California law, the Council shall indemnify its Officers, Executive Committee members, committee chairs, and authorized agents acting in good faith and in a manner they reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Council. The Council may maintain appropriate Errors & Omissions (E&O) insurance or professional liability coverage for the protection of its leadership and agents against claims arising from acts, errors, omissions, or performance of duties undertaken on behalf of the Council.

Section 3.08 VACANCIES Vacancies in any elected office or Member-at-Large position may be filled by vote of the Executive Committee for the unexpired portion of the term until the next regular election.
