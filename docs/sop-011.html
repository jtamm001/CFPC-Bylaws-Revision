California Forest Pest Council: 2026 Governance Framework & Bylaws Revision

SOP-011: CEU’s Guidance Draft

ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

  1. Purpose: This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes Section 1.02(5) (Educational Objectives) and Section 4.06(b) (Conference Committee) of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It provides precise rules for applying for, tracking, verifying, and reporting Continuing Education Units (CEUs) for licensed natural resource professionals attending Council meetings, workshops, and field tours.
  2. Accrediting Bodies Covered:
    • California Department of Pesticide Regulation (DPR): Pest Control Advisors (PCA), Qualified Applicator Licensees/Certificates (QAL/QAC), and Private Applicators (Laws & Regs, Other, Aerial, IPB).
    • International Society of Arboriculture (ISA / WCISA): Certified Arborists, Utility Specialists, Municipal Specialists, Board Certified Master Arborists (BCMA), and Tree Workers.
    • Society of American Foresters (SAF) / CA Board of Forestry: Category 1 Continuing Forestry Education (CFE) for Registered Professional Foresters (RPFs).

ARTICLE II. CEU COORDINATOR DUTIES & TIMELINE

The Conference Committee shall appoint a dedicated CEU Program Coordinator responsible for managing accrediting agency applications, compliance audits, and post-event roster filings.

Section 2.01 Application Timelines & Deadlines

Accrediting Agency Application Deadline Mandatory Application Materials
CA Department of Pesticide Regulation (DPR) Minimum 60 Days Prior Formal Agenda, Detailed Timed Outline, Presentation Abstracts, Speaker Bios/Resumes.
International Society of Arboriculture (ISA) Minimum 45 Days Prior Detailed Timed Agenda, Domain Topic Mapping (e.g., Tree Risk, Diagnosis, Biology).
Society of American Foresters (SAF / CFE) Minimum 30 Days Prior Course Syllabus, Instructor Qualifications, Instructional Hours Breakdown.

ARTICLE III. DPR AND ISA COURSE COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

Section 3.01 DPR Content & Categorization Guidelines 1. Laws & Regulations: Presentation content must cover California pesticide laws, worker safety, environmental protection, or regulatory updates. 2. Other / Management: Presentation content must focus directly on pest biology, monitoring, biological control, silvicultural pest management, or invasive species detection. 3. Prohibition of Commercial Sales: Presentations containing product sales pitches, commercial brand promotions, or non-scientific advertising are strictly prohibited from receiving DPR/ISA CEU accreditation.

Section 3.02 ISA / WCISA Domain Mapping 1. Session topics must be explicitly mapped to ISA domains (e.g., Tree Biology, Diagnosis & Treatment, Urban Forestry, Soil Management, Safe Work Practices). 2. Field tours must include structured instructional stops with designated demonstration leaders to qualify for ISA field CEUs.

ARTICLE IV. ON-SITE TRACKING AND ATTENDANCE VERIFICATION

To comply with audit mandates enforced by DPR and ISA, strict verification protocols must be maintained during all Council events:

  1. Roster Sign-In / Sign-Out Sheet Requirements:
    • Separate, explicit sign-in sheets must be maintained for DPR, ISA, and SAF/RPF.
    • Attendees must write legibly: Full Name, License/Certification Number, Sign-In Time, and Sign-Out Time.
    • Partial credit is strictly prohibited by DPR; attendees arriving more than 10 minutes late or leaving early shall not receive credit for that specific session.
  2. Course Code Distribution: Official DPR Approval Numbers and ISA Event Codes shall only be released to attendees at the conclusion of the qualified session or via certified email following roster verification.
  3. Field Tour Verification: For outdoor sessions, sign-in sheets shall be verified by designated field stop moderators at the beginning and end of the field circuit (SOP-005).

ARTICLE V. POST-EVENT REPORTING AND AUDIT RETENTION

  1. Reporting Deadlines:
    • DPR Roster Submission: Verified attendee lists must be submitted via the DPR online sponsor portal or official submission forms within fifteen (15) business days post-event.
    • ISA Roster Submission: ISA course rosters must be uploaded to the ISA sponsor portal within thirty (30) calendar days post-event.
  2. Completion Certificates: The CEU Coordinator shall issue digital Certificates of Completion to verified attendees within twenty-one (21) days following the meeting.
  3. Audit Record Retention: Pursuant to DPR regulations and SOP-007 (Record Retention), all physical sign-in sheets, approved agendas, speaker bios, and agency approval letters must be permanently retained in the CFPC digital archive for a minimum of three (3) years and made available for state audits upon request.
