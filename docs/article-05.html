ARTICLE V. THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

Section 5.01 ROLE, DUTIES, AND AUTHORITY The Executive Committee manages the operational affairs of the Council between membership meetings, coordinates the activities of the Council and its committees, reviews recommendations for Council action, oversees Council finances and grant administration, and serves as the official voice of the Council.

When taking action on matters within the scope of a specific committee, the Executive Committee shall seek input from the affected committee prior to taking action whenever practical.

For purposes of the California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law, the Executive Committee serves as the Board of Directors of the corporation.

Section 5.02 COMPOSITION AND APPOINTMENTS

(a) Officers: The Officers of the Council (Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer).

(b) Committee Representatives: The Chairperson of each Technical Committee, Administrative Committee, and Task Force.

(c) Members-at-Large: Three (3) Members-at-Large elected by the Council.

(d) Agency and Industry Representation Appointments: In the event the membership of the Executive Committee set forth in subsections (a) through (c) does not include at least one (1) representative each from: 1. The California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE); 2. The Pacific Southwest Region of the USDA Forest Service; 3. The University of California; 4. Private industry;

the Chairperson is authorized to appoint an additional person or persons as necessary to ensure representation from these organizations.

(e) Alternates: Each Executive Committee member may designate an alternate to attend an Executive Committee meeting on their behalf in the event they cannot attend. Because the Executive Committee serves as the Board of Directors under California law, designated alternates may participate in discussions but shall not have voting privileges and shall not count toward establishing a quorum.

Section 5.03 SCOPE OF AUTHORITY AND OPERATIONAL LIMITS To ensure efficient management while preserving fundamental corporate governance:

(a) Executive Committee Scope: The Executive Committee has full authority to: 1. Direct Council operations, manage grants, oversee corporate assets, and manage discretionary spending; 2. Adopt, amend, and enforce Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to govern overall Council operations, including financial approval thresholds for Executive Committee actions; 3. Review and approve Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines, and charters developed by sub-committees, working groups, or task forces; 4. Adopt official Council public policy positions, technical recommendations, public statements, and formal resolutions, or, at the Executive Committee’s discretion, submit significant policy matters to a vote of the general membership pursuant to Section 8.01(b); 5. Appoint committee chairs and fill interim leadership vacancies as authorized by these Bylaws; 6. Take emergency action on behalf of the Council when immediate action is necessary to protect the Council’s interests.

(b) Reserved Membership Authority: The Executive Committee shall not take the following actions without approval by a vote of the general membership: 1. Amending or repealing these Bylaws or the Articles of Incorporation; 2. Electing Officers and Members-at-Large; 3. Approving major structural corporate actions, such as a merger, dissolution, major reorganization, or selling/disposing of substantially all Council assets.

Section 5.04 VOTING AND QUORUM Each defined member of the Executive Committee shall be entitled to one (1) vote.

A quorum shall consist of fifty percent (50%) of the voting members of the Executive Committee. Unless otherwise provided in these Bylaws, actions of the Executive Committee shall be decided by a simple majority (50% plus one) vote of the voting members present.

Section 5.05 ACTION WITHOUT A MEETING AND ELECTRONIC VOTING (a) Action by Written Consent: Any action required or permitted to be taken by the Executive Committee may be taken without a meeting if all voting members of the Executive Committee consent in writing (including electronic mail) to the action.

(b) Electronic Meetings and Email Votes: In lieu of a physical meeting, the Chairperson may call an electronic or email vote on urgent or routine operational matters. Provided that a quorum of the Executive Committee submits written responses or votes via electronic transmission within a specified response window, the action shall be decided by a simple majority vote of those responding. All written consents, ballots, and electronic votes shall be filed with the minutes of the Executive Committee.
