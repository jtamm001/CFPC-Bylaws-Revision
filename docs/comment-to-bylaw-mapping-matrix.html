California Forest Pest Council: 2026 Governance Framework & Bylaws Revision

3 Comment-to-Bylaw Mapping Matrix

The table below maps every comment and internal tag directly to its final location and resolution in the revised Bylaws and supporting SOPs.

Source ID Section / Context Summary of Comment / Request Final Bylaws / SOP Resolution
Chris Lee Note Section 1.03 Add “and Fire Protection” to Board title. Standardized title across all sections as California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection.
[JT1] Section 4.01 Add umbrella definition for subgroup types to avoid repetitive listings throughout document. Defined Technical, Administrative, Ad Hoc, Task Forces, and Executive Committee collectively as “Committees.”
[JT2] Section 4.03 Clarify that SOPs can be adopted by each subgroup subject to EC approval. Added explicit SOP delegation rule requiring EC approval for all sub-committee SOPs/charters.
[JT3] Section 4.04 Separate subgroup structure into clear “elected” vs “appointed” tracks. Created Section 4.04(a)-(c) defining Technical (Elected), Administrative (Appointed), and Task Force tracks.
[JT4] Section 5.02(b) Clarify that only standing committee chairs hold EC seats, excluding temporary ad hoc chairs. Restricted voting EC seats to standing Technical, Administrative, and active Task Force chairs.
[JT5] / [JT6] Section 6.01 Streamline 2-year voting eligibility rules, Code of Ethics requirement, and registration options. Merged attendance and professional capacity registration under Section 6.01(a)-(b).
[JT7] / Ted Swiecki Section 6.02(b) Add flexible voting options (voice, show of hands, electronic, secret paper ballot) to prevent peer intimidation. Incorporated secret ballot motion rules under Robert’s Rules of Order in Section 6.02(b).
Chris Lee Note Section 4.06(a) Change “proceedings” to “minutes” or “summary” for committee publications. Updated Section 4.06(a) terminology to reflect annual meeting summaries and condition reports.
Chris Lee Note Section 5.02(d) Ensure USDA Forest Service wording isn’t restricted solely to “Regional” structure due to reorganization. Kept USFS designation flexible (Pacific Southwest Region / USDA Forest Service) to absorb administrative shifts.
Chris Lee Note Section 5.02(d) Expand higher education representation beyond UC to include CSU. Expanded academic appointment seat to include University of California (UC) and California State University (CSU) systems.
Chris Lee / Joelene Tamm Section 9.01, 9.06 Clarify accounting procedures, audit policies, and 2 CFR federal grant requirements. Referenced financial controls in Section 9.01, deferring specific audit mechanics to internal SOPs (SOP-004 & SOP-008) to maintain flexibility.
Joelene Tamm Note Preamble, 5.02(d) Recognize Tribal governments. Added explicit Tribal representation in Preamble and under Section 5.02(d).
Action Item (10/11/26) Section 2.01, 6.01 Danielle Lindler requested drafting a standalone CFPC Code of Ethics. Added as an active action item to operationalize member voting requirements established in Articles II and VI.
Stephen Jones Note SOP-004 (Art. II, § 3) & SOP-008 (Sec. 2.1) Flagged potential conflict between SOP-004 and SOP-008; noted quarterly bank statement/reconciliation reviews by a non-signatory EC member are excessive during low-activity periods. Completed in SOP-008 & SOP-004: Eliminated mandatory quarterly reviews and consolidated reconciliation oversight into the semi-annual (twice-yearly) review by two non-signatory EC members (with an interim trigger only if quarterly disbursements exceed $5,000).
Stephen Jones Note SOP-008 (Sec. 3, Tier 1) & SOP-004 (Art. VI, § 2) Questioned whether Tier 1 “biannual” (twice-yearly) review by two non-signatory EC members overlaps with quarterly reviews, or if “biennial” (every two years) was intended. Completed in SOP-008 & SOP-004: Clarified terminology as Semi-Annual (Twice-Yearly) internal review in SOP-008 (Section 3, Tier 1) and cross-referenced SOP-008 as primary authority in SOP-004 (Article VI, § 2).
Stephen Jones Note SOP-008 (Sec. 4) & SOP-004 (Art. VI, § 3) Noted omission of responsibility for annual government/grant filings (IRS, FTB, CA DOJ, CA SOS, SAM.gov, grant reports); suggested assigning oversight to the Vice-Chairperson alongside or in support of the Treasurer. Completed in SOP-008 & SOP-004: Added Section 4 to SOP-008 (cross-referenced in SOP-004 Article VI, § 3) designating the Vice-Chairperson as Compliance Coordinator to track and verify annual IRS, FTB, CA DOJ, CA SOS, SAM.gov, and grant accomplishment filings.
Kim Corella Note Section 7.03 Expressed concern that vague “periodically” wording could result in the Executive Committee skipping interim meetings and meeting less than twice per year. Replaced vague “periodically” language in Section 7.03 with a mandatory minimum of three (3) annual EC meetings: regular Spring and Fall meetings plus at least one (1) interim meeting.
