Article VI. VOTING RIGHTS

Section 6.01 VOTING ELIGIBILITY Voting eligibility is limited to members who agree to abide by the Council’s Code of Ethics.

Eligibility shall remain valid for two (2) years and may be established by either of the following: (a) Attendance: Attendance during the preceding two (2) years at a Council meeting, Technical or Administrative Committee meeting, field session, workshop, conference, or other Council-sponsored activity; or (b) Eligibility Request: Submission of a written or electronic request for voting eligibility to the Secretary, or same-day registration at a Council meeting, demonstrating an interest or involvement in forest health, forestry, natural resource management, pest management, research, education, fire protection, or related fields. Section 6.02 VOTING RULES Each eligible member shall be entitled to one (1) vote on each matter submitted to the general membership. Unless otherwise provided in these Bylaws, actions of the membership shall be decided by a majority vote of the voting members present. (a) Standard Voting: Voting at meetings shall generally be conducted by voice vote, show of hands, or appropriate electronic means. Any member may demand a verification of a voice vote by a show of hands. A formal, counted vote may be ordered by the Chairperson or by a majority vote of the members present. (b) Ballot or Recorded Voting: A vote shall be conducted by confidential ballot or recorded roll call if a motion to do so is made, seconded, and approved by a majority of the voting members present. The motion must specify whether the vote shall be confidential (anonymous) or recorded (identified). Ballots may be cast physically or through secure electronic platforms that support the requested method. Such a motion must be made before the final result of the standard vote is officially announced by the Chairperson. Section 6.03 ROSTERS The official roster of eligible voting members shall be maintained by the Council Secretary. To accommodate fluid participation, this roster is maintained as an ongoing record and may be updated during Council meetings to immediately grant voting rights to attendees who establish eligibility on-site. Each Technical and Administrative Committee shall maintain an ongoing roster of members participating in or interested in its subject area. Committees shall promptly provide roster updates to the Council Secretary following meetings, field sessions, or workshops.
