SOP-002: Committee Charter Template

1. PURPOSE AND MISSION Pursuant to Article IV (Section 4.03)** and Article V (Section 5.03) of the CFPC Bylaws, this Charter defines the scope, leadership structure, and operating procedures for the [Insert Sub-Committee Name] (herein referred to as the “Committee”).

Mission Statement: The Committee serves the Council by monitoring, evaluating, and addressing forest health priorities in the subject area of [Insert Key Focus Area, e.g., Forest Insects / Diseases / Pitch Canker / Conference Planning].

2. SUBGROUP TRACK AND LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE In accordance with Bylaws Section 4.04, this group operates under the following track:

  • [ ] Technical / Elected Track (§ 4.04a): Open membership; leadership elected by committee participants for a 2-year term.
  • [ ] Administrative / Appointed Track (§ 4.04b): Leadership appointed directly by the Executive Committee.
  • [ ] Task Force / Ad Hoc Track (§ 4.04c): Standing or temporary operational body; track assigned by the Executive Committee.

Sub-Committee Leadership Roles: * Committee Chair: [Insert Name / Agency or Corporate Affiliation] (Serves as voting representative on the Executive Committee per § 4.02) * Vice-Chair / Secretary: [Insert Name / Affiliation] (Responsible for roster maintenance and transmitting meeting minutes per § 7.06)

3. CORE RESPONSIBILITIES & DELIVERABLES 1. Annual Pest Conditions Input: Review conditions and assemble technical summaries for inclusion in the annual CFPC Forest Pest Conditions Report (§ 4.06a). 2. Annual Meeting Participation: Convene at least once annually prior to the Fall full Council meeting to formulate technical findings and policy recommendations (§ 7.03). 3. Resolution Drafting: Draft, review, and forward proposed technical resolutions or policy position statements to the Executive Committee for review (§ 8.01). 4. Roster Maintenance: Maintain an ongoing roster of active participants and promptly submit updates to the Council Secretary (§ 6.03).

4. MEETINGS, VOTING, AND REPORTING * Meetings: Conducted at least annually, governed by Robert’s Rules of Order (§ 7.05). * Voting Eligibility: Open to all registered participants who agree to abide by the Council’s Code of Ethics (SOP-001) and meet 2-year participation criteria (§ 6.01). * Minutes Transmission: Copies of all committee meeting minutes, technical reports, and formal correspondence shall be transmitted to the EC Secretary within thirty (30) days (§ 7.06).
