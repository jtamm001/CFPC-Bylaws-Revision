California Forest Pest Council: 2026 Governance Framework & Bylaws Revision

Comments Summary

This document serves as the official review and crosswalk for all revision comments, email correspondence, internal drafting tags ([JT1]–[JT7]), and regulatory notes submitted during the 2026 Bylaws update of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC).

Key contributors to this review include: - Chris Lee, Forest Pathologist, CAL FIRE - Ted Swiecki, Principal/Plant Pathologist, Phytosphere Research - Danielle Lindler, CEO / RPF #2691, Jefferson Resource Company, Inc. - Joelene Tamm, Natural Resources Director, La Jolla Band of Luiseño Indians - Stephen Jones, Former Long-Term Treasurer, CFPC - Kim Corella, Forest Pest Specialist, CAL FIRE

0.4 Detailed Analysis of Major Technical Comments

0.4.1 1. Voting Methods & Secret Ballot Safeguards (Ted Swiecki Analysis)

Issue: Concerns were raised regarding potential peer pressure or intimidation during public votes on controversial forest health policy issues or contested officer elections.

Resolution in Bylaws (Section 6.02 & Section 5.05): - Routine Decisions: Voice vote, show of hands, or digital polling for fast consensus. - Controversial Matters: Added an explicit procedural mechanism under Robert’s Rules of Order where any voting member can move for a confidential (secret) ballot or anonymous electronic vote.

0.4.2 2. Financial Controls, Audit Harmonization & Statutory Filings (Stephen Jones Analysis)

Issue: Review of preliminary financial SOPs identified overlapping bank reconciliation schedules (SOP-004 quarterly vs. SOP-008 biannual), ambiguous “biannual” wording, and unassigned ownership for annual tax, corporate, SAM.gov, and grant accomplishment filings.

Resolution in Bylaws (Section 3.03), SOP-008 (Primary Authority) & SOP-004: - Streamlined Bank Statement Reviews (SOP-008 § 2.1 & SOP-004 Art. II, § 3): Replaced the mandatory quarterly review (which is excessive during low-activity quarters) with a semi-annual (twice-yearly) review by two non-signatory EC members—directly aligned with the SOP-008 Tier 1 schedule (with additional interim reviews held only if requested by the EC). - Audit / Review Harmonization (SOP-008 § 3 & SOP-004 Art. VI, § 2): Designated SOP-008 as the master governance policy for internal controls, audits, and recordkeeping, clarifying that Tier 1 internal reviews occur semi-annually (twice per year) by two non-signatory EC members. - Vice-Chairperson Succession & Annual Filings (Section 3.03, SOP-008 § 4 & SOP-004 Art. VI, § 3): Added an explicit succession and compliance oversight duty to Section 3.03 of the Bylaws and created a compliance table in SOP-008 assigning the Vice-Chairperson as Compliance Coordinator to oversee and verify timely completion of IRS, CA Franchise Tax Board, CA Department of Justice, CA Secretary of State, SAM.gov, and grant accomplishment filings alongside the Treasurer and Project Managers.

0.4.3 3. Executive Committee Meeting Frequency Safeguards (Kim Corella Analysis)

Issue: Replacing the 1990s fixed-month EC meeting schedule (February, August, and November) with “periodically throughout the year” risked allowing future Executive Committees to skip interim meetings and convene only once prior to the Fall General Meeting.

Resolution in Bylaws (Section 7.03): - Minimum Seasonal Cadence: Mandated that the Executive Committee meet at least three (3) times annually—specifically holding regular Spring and Fall meetings plus at least one (1) interim meeting—preserving scheduling flexibility while preventing meeting omissions or back-to-back bunching.
