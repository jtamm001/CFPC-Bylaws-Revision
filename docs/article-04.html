Article IV. COMMITTEES

Section 4.01 COMMITTEES The Council shall conduct its activities through Technical Committees, Administrative Committees, Ad Hoc committees, Task Forces, and the Executive Committee (collectively referred to as “Committees”).

To maintain operational flexibility, the Executive Committee may establish, restructure, consolidate, rename, or dissolve Committees as necessary to fulfill the purposes of the Council.

Section 4.02 COMMITTEE REPRESENTATION ON EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE The Chairperson of each standing Technical Committee, Administrative Committee, and active Task Force shall serve as its designated representative on the Executive Committee. No individual shall cast more than one vote on the Executive Committee, regardless of the number of leadership roles held.

Section 4.03 STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES Each Committee is authorized to develop and maintain its own Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), guidelines, or charters to govern its internal activities, meetings, and leadership elections. These operational documents may be drafted and refined progressively over time. They shall take effect upon their independent review and approval by the Executive Committee, provided they do not conflict with these Bylaws.

Section 4.04 SUBGROUP STRUCTURE AND TRACKS The structure of Council subgroups shall align with their intended function:

  1. Technical / Elected Track: Technical Committees shall have open membership and elect their own leadership.
  2. Administrative / Appointed Track: Administrative Committees shall have their leadership appointed by the Executive Committee.
  3. Task Forces and Ad Hoc Committees: Upon establishing a Task Force or Ad Hoc committee, the Executive Committee shall designate its leadership structure under either the elected or appointed track.

Section 4.05 TECHNICAL COMMITTEES The Council shall maintain Technical Committees addressing core forest health priorities, including forest insects, forest diseases, invasive vegetation, animal damage, and Southern California forest health issues.

Section 4.06 ADMINISTRATIVE COMMITTEES The Council shall maintain the following Administrative Committees:

(a) The Editorial Committee: Responsible for arranging the preparation and publication of the annual forest pest conditions report, proceedings of the annual meeting, and other official publications. The Editorial Committee chair shall preferably be designated by the Director of the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection.

(b) The Conference Committee: Responsible for planning and executing the annual meeting of the California Forest Pest Council, including programming, local arrangements, registration, and publicity.

Section 4.07 TASK FORCES Task Forces address specific, complex, or ongoing forest health issues (e.g., Firewood Task Force, Pitch Canker Task Force) and operate as standing operational bodies dedicated to a focused area of concern.

Section 4.08 AD HOC COMMITTEES Ad Hoc committees are temporary bodies created for a specific task and shall automatically dissolve upon submitting their final findings or completing their designated objective.
