# ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes **Section 1.02(5)** (Objectives), **Section 1.03** (Advisory Role), **Section 3.02** (Duties of Chairperson), and **Article VIII** (Advocacy and Policy Positions) of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It establishes formal guidelines for public education, digital media management, press inquiries, multi-agency outreach, and Tribal government engagement.
2. **Governing Scope:** Applies to all Officers, Executive Committee members, Committee Chairs, Task Force leaders, and general Council participants representing the CFPC in public, academic, professional, or media settings.

---

# ARTICLE II. SPOKESPERSON AUTHORIZATION AND MEDIA RELATIONS

To ensure scientific accuracy, organizational neutrality, and compliance with corporate policy:

### Section 2.01 Designated Representatives (§ 3.02)
1. **Official Voice:** Only the Council Chairperson, or an Executive Committee member or authorized designee appointed in writing by the Chairperson, is authorized to speak publicly, issue press releases, or testify on behalf of the California Forest Pest Council.
2. **Individual Opinions & Disclaimers (SOP-001 § 2.02):** No individual member or committee participant shall represent their personal, professional, or employer policy as an official position of the CFPC. When expressing individual expert opinions in public settings, members must explicitly state that their views do not represent the official stance of the Council.

### Section 2.02 Media Inquiries Protocol
1. **Routing Inquiries:** All media requests (print, radio, television, digital journalists) regarding Council resolutions, annual forest pest conditions, or statewide pest emergencies must be forwarded immediately to the Chairperson and Secretary.
2. **Review Window:** Press statements addressing controversial forest health policies, pesticide applications, or quarantine actions must undergo Executive Committee review pursuant to **Bylaws Section 5.03(a)(4)** prior to public release.

# ARTICLE III. DIGITAL OUTREACH, WEBSITE, AND SOCIAL MEDIA

1. **CFPC Web Repository Management:** The Secretary, in coordination with the Conference and Editorial Committees, shall oversee the official CFPC website and public document repository.
2. **Core Website Deliverables:**
   * Archiving of all annual *California Forest Pest Conditions Reports* (**SOP-003**).
   * Host venue details, agenda programs, and online registration portals for the Fall Annual Meeting (**SOP-005**).
   * Current governance documents, including Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, and approved Standard Operating Procedures.
   * Educational resources, pest alerts, and technical publication links.
3. **Social Media & Electronic Messaging:** Official Council social channels (e.g., LinkedIn, YouTube for presentation recordings) shall be maintained by an authorized Digital Communications Coordinator designated by the Executive Committee. Posts shall strictly focus on scientific research, pest alerts, meeting notices, and educational announcements.

---

# ARTICLE IV. TRIBAL AND MULTI-AGENCY STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT

Recognizing the foundational role of Tribal sovereignty and multi-agency coordination in California natural resource management (**Bylaws Preamble & § 5.02d**):

1. **Tribal Government Outreach:** The Council shall proactively invite Tribal Natural Resource Departments, Tribal Forestry staff, and cultural resource specialists to participate in technical committees, field tours, and annual meetings.
2. **Inter-Agency Information Sharing:** The Council shall maintain active educational liaisons with partner organizations, including:
   * **California Board of Forestry and Fire Protection** (**§ 1.03**)
   * **CAL FIRE & USDA Forest Service Region 5 Forest Health Protection**
   * **University Extension Systems (UC Cooperative Extension, CSU)**
   * **Western Forest Insect Work Conference (WFIWC) & Western International Forest Disease Work Conference (WIFDWC)**

---

# ARTICLE V. EDUCATIONAL PUBLICATIONS AND FIELD DEMONSTRATIONS

1. **Educational Materials Development:** Technical Committees and Task Forces (e.g., Firewood Task Force, Pitch Canker Task Force) may develop pest alerts, field identification cards, and educational brochures.
2. **Scientific Integrity & Review:** All educational materials bearing the CFPC logo or name must undergo technical peer review by the appropriate Technical Committee Chair and final publication sign-off by the Editorial Committee (**SOP-003**).
3. **501(c)(3) Compliance Limits (§ 8.01c):** All outreach publications, educational presentations, and public statements shall strictly comply with lobbying restrictions and political intervention limits under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
