# SOP-009: Education, Public Outreach & Stakeholder Engagement {- #sop-009}

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the framework for how the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) communicates with the public, the media, and its institutional partners. As a living document, it establishes baseline guidelines for digital outreach, press inquiries, and collaborative engagement to ensure scientific accuracy and organizational neutrality. This policy applies to anyone representing the CFPC in a public, academic, or professional setting.

## 2. SPOKESPERSON & MEDIA RELATIONS
To ensure consistent messaging and protect the Council's 501(c)(3) status, all public representation must follow these guidelines:

*   **Official Voice:** The Council Chairperson (or a designated Executive Committee member) serves as the sole official spokesperson for the CFPC. All media requests regarding Council resolutions or statewide pest emergencies must be routed to the Chairperson.
*   **Individual Opinions:** Members and committee participants are encouraged to share their expertise publicly. However, when expressing personal or employer-specific opinions, members must explicitly state that their views do not represent the official stance of the CFPC.
*   **Press Releases:** Any official press statements addressing controversial forest health policies or regulatory actions must be reviewed by the Executive Committee prior to publication.

## 3. DIGITAL PRESENCE & EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS
The CFPC utilizes digital platforms and print media to fulfill its educational mission:

*   **Web Repository:** The CFPC website serves as the central hub for archiving the annual *California Forest Pest Conditions Report*, distributing meeting agendas, and hosting approved governance documents.
*   **Social Media:** Official social media channels will be strictly utilized for sharing scientific research, pest alerts, meeting notices, and relevant educational content. 
*   **Educational Publications:** Technical Committees and Task Forces may draft educational materials (e.g., pest identification cards, brochures). Before distribution under the CFPC name, these materials must undergo a technical review by the Editorial Committee to ensure scientific integrity.

## 4. LOBBYING RESTRICTIONS
As a registered public benefit corporation, all CFPC outreach, educational publications, and public statements must strictly comply with the political intervention and lobbying restrictions defined under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
