# SOP-001: Code of Ethics Draft {- #sop-001}

The California Forest Pest Council (CFPC), founded in 1951, is a cooperative organization composed of individuals and organizations committed to the health and stewardship of California's forests. Through education, scientific exchange, and collaboration, the Council promotes the understanding, prevention, detection, evaluation, and management of forest pests and other factors affecting forest health.

The Council serves as a forum for the exchange of scientific, technical, and practical knowledge and encourages informed decision-making related to forest health management. The Council recognizes that healthy forests provide important ecological, economic, cultural, and social benefits and that effective stewardship depends upon collaboration, sound science, and consideration of diverse perspectives.

To support this mission, members are expected to conduct themselves with honesty, objectivity, professionalism, and respect. This Code of Ethics establishes the principles that guide participation in Council activities and help ensure the Council's work remains credible and effective.

Principles

1. Stewardship of Forest Health

Members support the long-term health, resilience, and sustainability of California's forests. Council activities shall be guided by sound science and responsible stewardship in understanding and managing forest pests and other threats to forest health.

2. Scientific Integrity and Objectivity

Members shall support the use of sound science, accurate information, and professional judgment in Council activities. Members shall distinguish among facts, interpretations, opinions, and uncertainties and shall not knowingly misrepresent or distort information.

3. Professional Competence and Education

Members participate in Council activities within the limits of their expertise and strive to maintain and enhance their professional knowledge and skills. Members support learning, mentorship, scientific inquiry, and the exchange of information among colleagues, students, and stakeholders.

4. Objectivity

Members shall strive to be objective, fair, and evidence-based in their participation in Council activities. Members shall clearly distinguish personal or organizational views from positions officially adopted by the Council.

5. Collaboration

Effective forest pest management and forest health stewardship benefit from diverse expertise, experience, and knowledge. Members shall foster open communication, constructive dialogue, mutual learning, and respectful consideration of differing viewpoints while engaging with others in a professional, courteous, and respectful manner.

6. Integrity

Members shall conduct themselves honestly, ethically, and in good faith. Members are expected to uphold the integrity and purposes of the California Forest Pest Council and adhere to the Council's bylaws and adopted policies.

7. Professional Conduct in Meetings and Communications

Members shall participate in Council meetings and activities in a respectful, orderly, and constructive manner. Members shall follow the Council's adopted meeting procedures and rules of order and contribute to fair and productive discussion. Disagreements shall focus on issues, information, and ideas rather than individuals. Communications related to Council business, whether verbal, written, electronic, or through social media, shall be conducted with professionalism, courtesy, and respect.


Application

This Code applies to all members, officers, committee participants, and individuals participating in activities of the California Forest Pest Council. Members are expected to uphold these principles while participating in Council meetings, committees, educational programs, publications, communications, and other Council-sponsored activities.

Membership in the Council reflects a shared interest in advancing the understanding and management of forest pests and forest health. Members support the purposes and mission of the California Forest Pest Council by contributing to the exchange of knowledge, experience, and ideas and by participating in Council activities in a professional, respectful, and constructive manner.

Violations of this Code may be reviewed and addressed in accordance with the Council's bylaws, policies, and procedures.
