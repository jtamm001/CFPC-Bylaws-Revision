# SOP-004: Financial Approvals Draft {- #sop-004}

**ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes **Section 9.01**, **Section 9.03**, and **Section 9.06** of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It establishes formal internal fiscal controls, signature authority levels, contracting requirements, and grant management workflows to protect Council assets, prevent fraud, and satisfy federal, state, and private grant compliance parameters (including 2 CFR 200 Uniform Guidance and State of California grant terms).
2. **Scope:** This policy applies to all Officers, Executive Committee (EC) members, Technical and Administrative Committee Chairs, project managers, contractors, and agents authorized to receive, hold, commit, or disburse funds on behalf of the Council.

---

**ARTICLE II. GENERAL FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION**

1. **Fiscal Year:** The Council operates on a fiscal year beginning January 1 and ending December 31 (**Bylaws § 9.05**).
2. **Depository Accounts:** All funds shall be deposited promptly in financial institutions approved by the Executive Committee (**Bylaws § 9.02**). 
3. **Dual Control & Separation of Duties:**
   * No single individual shall have absolute control over the authorization, execution, and reconciliation of any financial transaction exceeding $1,000.00.
   * Bank statements and reconciliations shall be reviewed quarterly by a designated Executive Committee member who is not an authorized signatory on the bank account.
4. **Prohibition of Cash Disbursements:** All disbursements must be made via trackable electronic funds transfer (EFT), official bank check, or authorized Council credit instrument. Cash disbursements are strictly prohibited.

---

**ARTICLE III. EXPENDITURE APPROVAL THRESHOLDS**

To maintain operational agility while ensuring rigorous fiduciary oversight, commitment and disbursement authority thresholds are established as follows:

| Expenditure Level | Threshold Range | Required Authorization | Verification / Documentation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Minor Operational** | Up to $1,000.00 | Single Officer (Treasurer **OR** Chairperson) | Itemized receipt or invoice submitted to Treasurer. |
| **Tier 2: Intermediate / Contractual** | $1,000.01 – $5,000.00 | Dual Officers (Treasurer **AND** Chairperson) | Written contract, PO, or invoice signed by both Officers. |
| **Tier 3: Major Corporate** | Exceeding $5,000.00 | Formal Majority Vote of the Executive Committee | Approved EC meeting minutes or formal written consent record. |
| **Emergency Gap Allocation** | Up to $2,500.00 | Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + Treasurer | Emergency justification filed with EC within 5 business days. |

*Note: Artificially splitting purchases or invoices to bypass higher approval thresholds is strictly prohibited.*

---

**ARTICLE IV. GRANT EXPENDITURE AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICIES**

The Council frequently administers public and private grants (e.g., USDA Forest Service, CAL FIRE, foundation funds). All grant-related financial activities must strictly comply with the following parameters:

1. **Grant Budget Alignment:** Expenses charged to grant accounts must be explicitly allowable, allocable, and necessary under the specific grant agreement and applicable regulations (e.g., 2 CFR 200 Subpart E for federal pass-through grants).
2. **Grant Disbursement Approvals:**
   * Reimbursement requests or direct project payments funded by grants must be certified by the project manager/committee chair and approved by the Treasurer prior to submission or disbursement.
   * Grant disbursements exceeding $5,000.00 require EC notification and verification that funds have been drawn down or secured from the grantor.
3. **Travel and Expense Reimbursements (§ 9.06):**
   * Reimbursements for reasonable, actual incidental expenses (e.g., annual meeting supplies, field tour transport, speaker travel) require prior written authorization from the EC or Chairperson.
   * Standard state/federal mileage rates (IRS standard) shall apply for vehicle travel. Itemized original receipts are required for all non-mileage claims exceeding $25.00.

---

**ARTICLE V. CONTRACTING, PROCUREMENT, AND GIFTS**

1. **Contract Execution Authority (§ 9.03):**
   * No individual may bind the Council to any contract or financial obligation without explicit authorization under Article III of this SOP.
   * Contracts exceeding $10,000.00 or spanning multiple fiscal years must undergo formal legal or financial review prior to Executive Committee approval and execution by the Chairperson.
2. **Competitive Bidding Guidelines:**
   * Purchases or vendor contracts funded by public grants exceeding $10,000.00 require at least two (2) competitive written quotes or a documented sole-source justification approved by the EC.
3. **Acceptance of Gifts and Non-Liquid Assets (§ 9.04):**
   * Unencumbered cash donations or contributions may be accepted by the Treasurer or Chairperson.
   * Any gift, bequest, or contribution of non-liquid assets requiring liquidation or ongoing management (e.g., real estate, equipment, vehicles, stock) must be formally reviewed and approved by a majority vote of the Executive Committee prior to legal acceptance.

---

**ARTICLE VI. FINANCIAL REPORTING AND AUDIT PROCEDURES**

1. **Regular Financial Reports:** The Treasurer shall present a comprehensive financial report (including income statements, balance sheets, and grant fund balances) at each regular Executive Committee meeting and at the Annual General Membership Meeting (**Bylaws § 3.05**).
2. **Annual Audit / Financial Review:**
   * The Executive Committee shall appoint an independent Financial Review Committee or external certified CPA to conduct an annual review or audit of the Council’s financial records.
   * Audit procedures shall adapt dynamically to evolving state and federal accounting standards without requiring Bylaw amendments.
3. **Record Retention:** All accounting records, vouchers, contracts, grant awards, and bank statements shall be retained for a minimum of seven (7) years following the close of the applicable fiscal year or grant closure.
