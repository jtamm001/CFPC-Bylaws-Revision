# ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes **Section 9.01** (Expenditures & Disbursements) and **Section 9.06** (Compensation and Reimbursement) of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It provides precise rules for officer/agent expense reimbursements, travel allowable costs, and the administration of CFPC Student & Tribal Travel Scholarships for meeting attendance.
2. **Regulatory Standards:**
   * **Bylaws Section 9.06:** Officers, EC members, and committee chairs serve without compensation but may be reimbursed for reasonable, actual incidental expenses incurred in support of Council business.
   * **2 CFR 200 Subpart E (§ 200.475):** Federal Travel Regulations (FTR) and GSA per diem standards governing travel charged against public grants (e.g., USDA Forest Service, CAL FIRE grants).
   * **IRS Publication 463:** Accountable plan requirements for tax-free expense reimbursements.

# ARTICLE II. ALLOWABLE TRAVEL EXPENSES AND RATES

Reimbursements are governed by strict accountable plan rules. Unsubstantiated claims or lavish/extravagant expenses will not be paid or reimbursed.

### Section 2.01 Vehicle Mileage and Ground Transportation
1. **Personal Vehicle Mileage:** Reimbursed at the current IRS Standard Mileage Rate in effect on the date of travel. Mileage claims must document point of origin, destination, and business purpose.
2. **Commercial Ground Transport:** Rental cars, taxis, rideshare services (e.g., Uber/Lyft), and airport shuttles are reimbursable when necessary for Council business. Compact/mid-size rental classes must be utilized unless pre-approved by the Treasurer for transporting meeting/field equipment.

### Section 2.02 Lodging, Airfare, and Per Diem Meals
1. **Lodging:** Reimbursed up to the single-occupancy room rate at the designated conference venue or current GSA/State of California per diem rate for the location. Itemized hotel folios showing zero balance are required.
2. **Commercial Airfare:** Economy/coach class airfare only. Premium economy, business, or first-class upgrades are non-reimbursable unless explicitly required for documented medical accommodations.
3. **Meals and Incidentals (M&IE):** Charged against GSA per diem rates for the specific travel destination. Meals provided directly by the CFPC conference (e.g., hosted field tour lunches or banquets) must be deducted from the daily per diem claim.

# ARTICLE III. TRAVEL SCHOLARSHIPS FOR MEETING ATTENDANCE

To promote inclusivity, professional development, and student/Tribal participation (**Bylaws Preamble & § 2.01**), the Council may allocate budget for Travel Scholarships to attend the CFPC Annual Meeting or technical field sessions.

### Section 3.01 Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Applicants:** Undergraduate/graduate students, early-career researchers, Tribal youth/natural resource technicians, or non-profit specialists presenting research or seeking career development in forest health.
2. **Good Standing:** Applicants must agree to abide by the Council Code of Ethics (**SOP-001**) and submit a complete scholarship application.

### Section 3.02 Award Structure and Application Workflow
1. **Annual Budget Allocation:** The Executive Committee shall establish the total scholarship budget during the annual budget review under **SOP-002**.
2. **Award Package:** Scholarships may cover student registration waiver, shared hotel lodging, and up to $500.00 in travel/stipend assistance.
3. **Selection Committee:** Applications shall be reviewed and awarded by a panel consisting of the Conference Committee Chair, Vice-Chair, and one designated Member-at-Large.

# ARTICLE IV. CLAIM SUBMISSION AND DISBURSEMENT WORKFLOW

To comply with IRS Accountable Plan rules, expense reimbursement packages must follow a strict submission window:

1. **Submission Deadline:** All expense reimbursement claims and scholarship travel vouchers must be submitted to the Treasurer within **thirty (30) calendar days** following the conclusion of travel or event. Claims submitted after 60 days will be forfeited unless an extension is granted by the Executive Committee.
2. **Required Documentation:**
   * Completed and signed **CFPC Travel Expense Claim Form**.
   * Itemized original receipts for all lodging, commercial transport, registration fees, and non-mileage claims exceeding $25.00.
   * Proof of event attendance or presentation (e.g., badge copy, program listing).
3. **Approval Thresholds (SOP-002 Alignment):**
   * **Up to $1,000.00:** Single approval by Treasurer.
   * **$1,000.01 – $5,000.00:** Dual approval by Treasurer and Chairperson.
   * **Over $5,000.00 / Unbudgeted Exceptions:** Full Executive Committee approval required.
