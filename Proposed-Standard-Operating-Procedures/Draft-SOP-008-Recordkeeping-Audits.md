# SOP-008: Recordkeeping, Internal Controls & Audit Procedures {- #sop-008}

## 1. PURPOSE & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the California Forest Pest Council’s (CFPC) internal financial controls, audit protocols, and record retention rules. It is designed to be easily followed by volunteers while maintaining strict compliance with California Nonprofit Law, state grant guidelines, and federal Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200).

---

## 2. INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS

To protect Council assets, prevent fraud, and maintain a clear audit trail, the following separation-of-duties protocols apply to all operations:

### 2.1 Cash Management & Purchasing
*   **No Cash Disbursements:** The Council does not pay vendors, distribute stipends, or reimburse members with physical cash without Executive Committee approval. All outbound payments must be made via check, electronic funds transfer (EFT), or authorized payment processor.
*   **No Petty Cash:** The Council does not maintain a petty cash box. For small, immediate event purchases (e.g., coffee, minor supplies), authorized members should use an official CFPC payment card or purchase out-of-pocket and submit a receipt for prompt reimbursement.
*   **Cash Receipts (Safety Gates):** When physical cash is collected (e.g., event registration, merchandise), it must be counted by two individuals (including at least one Executive Committee member or event chair), logged immediately, and deposited *intact* into the CFPC bank account within three (3) business days.
*   **Reconciliation & Verification:** Every payment requires an itemized receipt or invoice. The Treasurer reconciles the bank accounts monthly, and a non-signatory Executive Committee member reviews those statements quarterly.

### 2.2 Expenditure Approval Thresholds
Authorizations for expenditures and disbursements are tiered as follows:
*   **Under $1,000:** Single Officer signature or electronic approval (Treasurer OR Chairperson).
*   **$1,000 to $5,000:** Dual Officer signature or electronic approval (Treasurer AND Chairperson).
*   **Over $5,000:** Requires a formal Executive Committee majority vote prior to execution.

## 3. AUDIT & REVIEW PROTOCOLS

Audit procedures scale with the Council’s funding levels to balance accountability with practical operational efficiency:

*   **Tier 1: Biannual Internal Review (Standard Operations)**
    For standard operations, the Executive Committee shall appoint at least two non-signatory members to conduct a biannual review of the Treasurer’s ledgers. This team will randomly sample transactions to verify receipts and ensure approval thresholds are being met.
*   **Tier 2: Independent CPA Audit**
    A formal audit by a Certified Public Accountant is only commissioned when explicitly required by an executed grant agreement, or when CFPC's gross revenue exceeds the California statutory threshold requiring an independent audit.
*   **Tier 3: Federal Single Audit**
    Conducted only if the Council expends $750,000 or more in federal grant funds (direct or pass-through) during a single fiscal year, strictly complying with 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.

---

## 4. RECORD RETENTION SCHEDULE

To maintain organizational continuity, records must be retained according to the following schedule:

*   **Permanent:** Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, IRS/State tax exemption letters, and official meeting minutes.
*   **7 Years:** Financial ledgers, bank statements, canceled checks, tax returns (e.g., IRS Form 990), and vendor invoices.
*   **3 Years Post-Closeout:** Grant records, data deliverables, and associated correspondence. These are kept for three years *after* the final grant expenditure report is submitted, unless the grantor requires a longer period.
