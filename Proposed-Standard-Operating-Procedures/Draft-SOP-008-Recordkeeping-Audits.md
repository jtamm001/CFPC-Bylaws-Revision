# ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY

1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes **Section 3.05** (Duties of Treasurer), **Section 9.01** (Expenditures & Disbursements), and **Section 9.06** of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It establishes formal rules for internal financial controls, annual audit protocols, grant accounting compliance, and record retention schedules.
2. **Regulatory Standards:** Designed to comply with:
   * **California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law** (Corp. Code § 5210 et seq.).
   * **2 CFR 200 Uniform Guidance** (Subpart E - Cost Principles & Subpart F - Audit Requirements) for federal pass-through grants (e.g., USDA Forest Service).
   * **State of California Grant Guidelines** (CAL FIRE & Natural Resources Agency mandates).

# ARTICLE II. INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS AND PREVENTATIVE MEASURES

To prevent unauthorized expenditures, fraud, or misallocation of Council and grant assets, the following separation-of-duties protocols are strictly enforced:

### Section 2.01 Dual Control & Cash Management
1. **No Cash Policy:** Cash transactions are prohibited. All receipts and disbursements shall occur via trackable bank electronic transfer (EFT), check, or merchant processor.
2. **Bank Account Reconciliation:** The Treasurer shall reconcile bank accounts monthly. A non-signatory Executive Committee member shall independently review and sign off on bank statements and reconciliations quarterly.
3. **Receipt Verification:** Every payment or reimbursement claim must be supported by an original itemized receipt, contract, or verified vendor invoice.

### Section 2.02 Dual-Signature Thresholds (SOP-002 Alignment)
* **Under $1,000.00:** Single Officer signature (Treasurer OR Chairperson).
* **$1,000.01 – $5,000.00:** Dual Officer signature (Treasurer AND Chairperson).
* **Exceeding $5,000.00:** Formal Executive Committee majority approval required prior to execution.
