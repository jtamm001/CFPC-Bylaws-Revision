# 3. CORE RESPONSIBILITIES & DELIVERABLES
1. **Annual Pest Conditions Input:** Review conditions and assemble technical summaries for inclusion in the annual CFPC Forest Pest Conditions Report (**§ 4.06a**).
2. **Annual Meeting Participation:** Convene at least once annually prior to the Fall full Council meeting to formulate technical findings and policy recommendations (**§ 7.03**).
3. **Resolution Drafting:** Draft, review, and forward proposed technical resolutions or policy position statements to the Executive Committee for review (**§ 8.01**).
4. **Roster Maintenance:** Maintain an ongoing roster of active participants and promptly submit updates to the Council Secretary (**§ 6.03**).

---

# 4. MEETINGS, VOTING, AND REPORTING
* **Meetings:** Conducted at least annually, governed by *Robert's Rules of Order* (**§ 7.05**).
* **Voting Eligibility:** Open to all registered participants who agree to abide by the Council's Code of Ethics (SOP-001) and meet 2-year participation criteria (**§ 6.01**).
* **Minutes Transmission:** Copies of all committee meeting minutes, technical reports, and formal correspondence shall be transmitted to the EC Secretary within thirty (30) days (**§ 7.06**).
