# SOP-006: Voting and Meetings {- #sop-006}

**ARTICLE I. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING AUTHORITY**

1. **Purpose:** This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) operationalizes **Section 5.05** (Action Without a Meeting & Electronic Voting) and **Section 6.02** (Voting Rules & Ballot Voting) of the California Forest Pest Council (CFPC) Bylaws. It provides precise rules for conducting electronic meetings, asynchronous email votes, live digital polling, and confidential (secret) balloting to ensure full legal compliance with California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law (Corp. Code § 5211).
2. **Core Objectives:**
   * Protect members from peer pressure, institutional conflict, or political coercion during contentious votes.
   * Maintain fluid, modern operational flexibility between annual meetings.
   * Ensure strict verification of "one-member, one-vote" integrity across virtual and in-person settings.

---

**ARTICLE II. ASYNCHRONOUS EMAIL VOTES & WRITTEN CONSENT (§ 5.05)**

When emergency or routine operational decisions must be made by the Executive Committee between regular meetings, the Chairperson may initiate an asynchronous email vote or written consent action under **Bylaws Section 5.05**.

**Section 2.01 Unanimous Written Consent vs. Electronic Majority Vote
1. **Unanimous Written Consent Action (§ 5.05a):** Pursuant to California Corp. Code § 5211(b), any action taken without a meeting and without advance meeting notice requires the explicit written consent (via email or signed ballot) of **100% of all voting members** of the Executive Committee.
2. **Electronic / Email Meeting Vote (§ 5.05b):** In lieu of a physical gathering, the Chairperson may issue a formal "Notice of Electronic Meeting and Action." Provided that a quorum (50%) of voting EC members submits written ballots within the designated response window, the action shall pass by a **simple majority** of those responding.

**Section 2.02 Standard Email Voting Workflow
1. **Initiation:** The Secretary or Chair shall distribute the motion, supporting documents, and an official ballot via electronic mail to all EC members.
2. **Discussion Window:** A minimum window of **forty-eight (48) hours** shall be provided for electronic discussion prior to the ballot closing, unless an emergency gap expenditure (§ 9.01) requires a expedited 24-hour window.
3. **Ballot Recording:** All email responses ("Approve," "Disapprove," "Abstain") shall be sent to the Secretary, compiled into a formal Voting Record, and filed permanently with the Executive Committee minutes (**Bylaws § 5.05b**).

---

**ARTICLE III. LIVE DIGITAL POLLING AND HYBRID MEETINGS**

For full Council meetings and Executive Committee sessions held via video conference (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Zoom) or hybrid formats:

1. **Roster Synchronization:** Prior to calling a live vote, the Secretary shall verify that attendees match the official 2-year voting eligibility roster maintained under **Bylaws Section 6.03**.
2. **Standard Voice / Show of Hands Voting (§ 6.02a):** Non-controversial motions may be decided by voice vote ("Viva Voce"), digital hand-raise, or unweighted chat response.
3. **Counted Voice Verification:** Any eligible voting member may demand an immediate counted verification of a voice vote by a physical or digital show of hands.

---

**ARTICLE IV. CONFIDENTIAL (SECRET) BALLOTING PROCEDURES (§ 6.02b)**

To uphold the safeguards established during the 2026 Bylaws revision, any member has the procedural right to protect their vote from public view on contentious policy issues, elections, or sensitive resolutions.
