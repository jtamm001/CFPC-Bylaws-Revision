## ARTICLE 10 AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS OR ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION{-}

**SECTION 1. Amendments.** 
Changes to the Bylaws or Articles of Incorporation shall be presented to the executive committee for review and then presented to the full Council at the next regularly scheduled business meeting. 

The By-laws and Articles of Incorporation may be amended by a majority vote of the eligible voting members present at the business meeting.
