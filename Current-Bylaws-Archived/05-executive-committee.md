## ARTICLE 4 COMMITTEES{-}

### **SECTION 5. The Executive Committee.**
The executive committee shall be composed of 15 members, as follows:
1. Executive committee chairperson - Council chairperson
2. Executive committee vice-chairperson - Council vice-chairperson
3. Executive committee secretary - Council secretary
4. Executive committee treasurer - Council treasurer
5. Member - animal damage committee chairperson
6. Member - disease committee chairperson
7. Member - insect committee chairperson
8. Member - weed committee chairperson
9. Member - Southern California committee chairperson
10. Members - editorial committee chairperson & editor-in-chief
11. Member - conference committee chairperson
12. Three members - at large, elected annually by the Council.

### **SECTION 6.** 
In the event the above membership of the executive committee does not include one member each representing the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection, the Pacific Southwest Region of the USDA Forest Service, the University of California, and private industry, the Chairperson is authorized to appoint an additional person or persons as necessary to represent these organizations.

### **SECTION 7.** 
Each executive committee member may designate an alternate to represent their interests in the event they can not attend an executive committee meeting.

### **SECTION 8.** 
The executive committee shall coordinate all activities of the Council and its committees, and review recommendations for Council action made by the committees. It shall be empowered to take emergency action on behalf of the Council when necessary. No action may be taken on a matter which concerns the subject area of a standing committee unless the standing committee's representative is present.

### **SECTION 9.**
The following 11 members of the executive committee have voting rights for matters that the executive committee votes upon:
1. Executive committee chairperson - Council chairperson
2. Executive committee vice-chairperson - Council vice-chairperson
3. Executive committee secretary - Council secretary
4. Member - animal damage committee chairperson
5. Member - disease committee chairperson
6. Member - insect committee chairperson
7. Member - weed committee chairperson
8. Member - Southern California committee chairperson
9. Three members - at large

Six voting members shall constitute a quorum if a representative from at least three of the following standing committees are present: animal damage, disease, insect, weed and Southern California.

### **SECTION 10.**
Executive committee members shall serve without compensation.

### **SECTION 11.** 
The executive committee will serve as the advisory committee to the Board of Forestry.

### **SECTION 12.**
Ad hoc committees may be organized and serve at the pleasure of the Council chairperson or the executive committee.
