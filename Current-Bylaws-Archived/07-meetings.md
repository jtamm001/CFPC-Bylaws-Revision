## ARTICLE 6 NOTICE OF MEETINGS{-}
**SECTION 1.** 
The Council secretary shall prepare and distribute notices of Council meetings to the membership.
**SECTION 2.** Standing committee chairpersons or secretaries shall handle distribution of notices of their meetings to those interested, sending copies also to members of the executive committee.
## ARTICLE 7 MEETINGS{-}
**SECTION 1. Full Council Meetings.** The full Council shall meet at least annually in November. The animal damage, disease, insect, weed and Southern California committees shall meet at least annually, and prior to the annual Council meeting, to review pest conditions in their subject area and to develop recommendations thereon to the Council.
**SECTION 2. Executive committee meetings.** 
Regular meetings of the executive committee shall be held in February, August and November. In November, the executive committee shall meet just prior to the annual Council meeting, to review and coordinate recommendations being submitted by committees to the Council.

**SECTION 3. Conduct of Meetings.** 
Meetings of the Council and committees shall be presided over by the appropriate chairperson. Meetings shall be governed by "Roberts' Rules of Order", revised version.
**SECTION 4. Minutes.** 
The Council secretary shall prepare and distribute minutes of Council meetings within thirty days thereof to members of the executive committee. Copies of the minutes may be distributed to other members with notices or at any subsequent meetings. The Council secretary shall send copies of resolutions or other important material being submitted to external bodies, as a result of Council action, to members of the executive committee.
The chairpersons or secretaries of the animal damage, disease, insect, weed and Southern California committees shall distribute copies of any minutes prepared of their meetings, and of any other pertinent correspondences and material, to the Secretary of the executive committee.
