## ARTICLE 9 FINANCES{-}

**SECTION 1. Expenditures.** 
The executive committee may authorize expenditures of Council funds. Checks, orders for the payment of money, and other evidence of indebtedness of the Council shall be signed by the treasurer, or Council chairman, or other person designated by the Council chairman. The Council chairman may authorize the reimbursement of reasonable incidental expenses in support of Council business.


**SECTION 2. Deposits.** 
All funds of the Council shall be deposited from time to time to the credit of the Council in such banks, trust companies, or other depositories as the executive committee, or other person designated by the Council chairman may select.

**SECTION 3. Contracts.**
The executive committee may authorize any officer or agent of the Council to enter into a contract on behalf of the Council. Unless so authorized, no officer, agent, or employee shall have any power or authority to bind the Council to any contract.

**SECTION 4. Gifts.**
The Council chairman or the treasurer may accept on behalf of the Council any contribution, gift, or bequest for the charitable or public purposes of this Council.

**SECTION 5. Fiscal year.**
The Council fiscal year shall begin on the 1st of January and end on the last day of December. *(Note: Document original misnumbered this as Section 3; corrected for sequence).*
