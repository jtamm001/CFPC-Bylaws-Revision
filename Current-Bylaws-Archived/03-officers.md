## ARTICLE 3 OFFICERS{-}

### **SECTION 1.**
The Council shall elect from its membership for a two year term of office a chairperson, vice-chairperson, and secretary. 
A treasurer, conference chairman and editorial chairman will be appointed annually by the Council chairperson to serve a one year term.

### **SECTION 2. Duties of the Chairperson.** 
The chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Council and the executive committee and shall perform any other duties or functions authorized by the executive committee. 
The chairperson may appoint ad hoc committees and their chairpersons, as deemed necessary to assist in carrying out the purposes of the Council. 
The chairperson is a non-voting member of all committees of the Council. 

The Council Chairperson or other designated member of the executive committee or Council shall represent the Council, and report forest pest conditions and recommendations of the Council to the State Board of Forestry and appear on behalf of the Council before any other public bodies. 

The Council Chairperson or other designated member of the executive committee or Council shall represent the Council, and report to, the annual meeting of the Western Forest Pest Committee of the Western Forestry and Conservation Association.

### **SECTION 3. Duties of Vice-Chairperson.**
In the absence of the chairperson, or in the event of his or her inability or refusal to act, the vice-chairperson shall perform all the duties of the chairperson.

### **SECTION 4. Duties of Secretary.** 
The secretary shall be custodian of the records and keep the original, and a copy of these Bylaws, a book of minutes of all meetings of the executive committee, standing committees and Council business meetings. The secretary should also keep a copy of all conditions reports, proceedings and other publications distributed by the Council. 
The secretary shall take minutes at the executive committee meetings and Council business meetings and distribute them to the executive committee within thirty days after the meeting. The secretary shall see that all meeting notices are distributed in a timely fashion and maintain the membership list.

### **SECTION 5. Duties of Treasurer.** 
The treasurer shall receive all payments, be custodian of funds of the Council, keep an account of all monies received and expended, and make commitments and disbursements authorized by the executive committee. 
At the annual business meeting and when asked by the executive committee, the treasurer shall make a full report covering the financial affairs of the Council. 
All funds, books, records and vouchers in the treasurer's control should be subject to inspection and verification by the executive committee. 
The treasurer shall deliver to their successor all books, records, money and accounts pertaining to or belonging to the Council.

### **SECTION 6. Compensation.**
Council officers shall serve without compensation.

### **SECTION 7. Non-liability of Officers.** 
The officers shall not be personally liable for the debts, liabilities, or other obligations of the Council.
