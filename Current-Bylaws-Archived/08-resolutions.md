## ARTICLE 8. RESOLUTIONS {-}
**SECTION 1. Resolutions.**
Resolutions are the means by which the Council advocates action or states positions on forest pest and forest health matters. 
Resolutions shall be introduced for discussion and adoption in the appropriate subject area committee. The Committee chairperson will forward the resolution to the executive committee for review. The Committee chairperson or his or her designee will then present the resolution at the annual business meeting so the membership may discuss and vote on the resolution.
Resolutions shall include the names and addresses of intended recipients.

Emergency resolutions may be presented to the executive committee for a vote if the resolution deals with an urgent matter that can not wait until the November meeting of the full council.
