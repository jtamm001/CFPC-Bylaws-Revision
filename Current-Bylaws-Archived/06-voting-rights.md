## ARTICLE 5 VOTING RIGHTS{-}

**SECTION 1.** 

Voting rights at the annual Council meeting and standing committee meetings will be established by attendance at the previous year's Council or standing committee meeting, by attendance at a field meeting of a standing committee the current year, or by application to the Council secretary sixty days prior to the annual Council meeting. Any rights so established will remain valid for two years without further participation.
Each member is entitled to one vote on each matter submitted to a vote by the members. Voting at meetings shall be by voice vote with majority rules. Election of Officers, however, shall be by ballot if the results of the voice vote are not clear. Each member of a standing committee shall vote for officers of only one committee.
 
 
 
 
 

**SECTION 2. Rosters.**
The Council secretary shall maintain and annually distribute to members of the executive committee:
1. Names and addresses of standing committee officers and of the full membership of the conference, editorial and executive committee.
2. Names and addresses of voting members thirty days prior the annual meeting of the Council.

The animal damage, disease, insect, weed and Southern California committees shall prepare and maintain lists of members interested in their subject matter field, and send copies to the Council secretary.
