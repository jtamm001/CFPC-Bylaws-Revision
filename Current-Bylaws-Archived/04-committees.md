## ARTICLE 4 COMMITTEES{-}

### **SECTION 1. Standing Committees.** 
Standing committees are established as follows:
- Animal Damage Committee
- Disease Committee
- Insect Committee
- Weed Committee
- Southern California Committee
- Editorial Committee
- Conference Committee
- Executive Committee






### **SECTION 2.** 
Membership on the Animal Damage, Disease, Insect, Weed and Southern California Committees shall be open to anyone interested in the subject matter of the Committee. The Animal Damage, Disease, Insect, Weed and Southern California Committees shall elect annually, from their membership, a chairperson, and a secretary. 

### **SECTION 3. The Editorial Committee.** 

The editorial committee shall be responsible for arranging for preparation and publication of an annual forest pest conditions report, proceedings of the annual CFPC meeting, and any other printed material of interest. The editorial committee will be chaired by one member representing the Director, California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection. The chairperson of the editorial committee will appoint the editor-in-Chief and any other members as needed.

### **SECTION 4. The Conference Committee.** 
The conference committee with the assistance of the Council shall plan and carry out an effective annual meeting of the California Forest Pest Council. It shall consist of a chairperson and members to handle the program, local arrangements, registration, publicity and other matters. The conference committee chairperson is appointed by the Council chairperson. The conference chairperson may then appoint all committee members.
